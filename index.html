📖 Bible Study

Welcome to our Presbyterian Church of Ghana Bible Study page.

Read God's Word, reflect on its message, and grow in faith.

Lesson 1: Faith in God

Scripture: Hebrews 11:1

Faith means trusting God even when we cannot see what lies ahead.

Questions to Think About

  1. What does faith mean to you?
  2. How can you show faith in your daily life?
  3. What can we learn from the people of faith in the Bible?

Prayer: Lord, strengthen our faith and help us trust You every day. Amen.

Lesson 2: Loving Your Neighbour

Scripture: Mark 12:31

Jesus teaches us to love our neighbours as ourselves.

Questions to Think About

  1. Who is your neighbour?
  2. How can you show kindness to someone this week?
